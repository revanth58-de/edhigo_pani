🌾 Dinasari Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026 | Compliant with DPDP Act 2023 (India)

Edhigo Pani Technologies Private Limited ("Dinasari", "We", "Us", or "Our") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users ("You", "User", "Farmer", "Worker", or "Group Leader"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, use, store, and share your personal data when you use the Dinasari mobile application and services (collectively, the "Services").

This policy has been updated to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

1. Consent and Your Rights

By registering and using the Services, you provide your explicit, specific, unconditional, and informed consent to the collection and processing of your personal data as described in this policy.

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the following rights as a "Data Principal":

  • Right to Access: You can request a summary of your personal data being processed and the processing activities.
  • Right to Correction and Erasure: You can request the correction of inaccurate details or the erasure of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our Grievance Officer.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide and optimize our agricultural matchmaking Services:

  • Registration Data: Full name, gender, age, profile photograph, and mobile number.
  • Role Selection: Whether you are registering as a Farmer, Worker, Group Leader, or Machinery Owner.
  • Location Data: Precise GPS coordinates of your location (collected in the foreground and, with your permission, background) to match farmers with local workers, enable navigation, and verify attendance.
  • Activity & Usage Data: Check-in/check-out timestamps via QR codes, work histories, ratings, and feedback reviews.
  • Transaction Data: UPI IDs or bank accounts used for processing and tracking payments.
  • Device Information: IP address, device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Personal Data

  • Matchmaking & Services: To match farmers with workers, show farm locations, and display list categories.
  • Attendance Verification: To confirm worker check-ins and check-outs on the farm using location and QR codes.
  • Communication: To send real-time notifications, OTP SMS, and updates via our messaging services.
  • Dispute Resolution: To resolve payment or performance disputes using activity logs and location histories.
  • Trust & Safety: To prevent fraud, verify identities, and ensure no underage labor uses the platform.

4. Data Sharing and Transfer

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We share your data only with:

  • Other Users: Farmers will see workers' names and ratings; workers will see farmers' names and locations to facilitate work.
  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors providing SMS services (OTP), cloud hosting, map APIs, and push notifications.
  • Legal Compliance: Law enforcement or government regulatory bodies when legally required to do so under Indian laws.

5. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards (including JWT tokens, encryption, and secure HTTPS channels) to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
