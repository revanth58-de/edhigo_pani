# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find answers to common questions about using Dinasari below.

---

## 🔑 General Questions

### 1. Is Dinasari free to download and use?
Yes, Dinasari is free to download. We may introduce small convenience fees or commissions for using matchmaking services in the future, which will be clearly displayed before any transaction.

### 2. Can I switch roles (e.g. from Farmer to Worker) in the app?
Yes! You can easily switch your dashboard mode at any time. Go to the **Profile** screen, slide the toggle button at the bottom, and confirm the switch.

### 3. What languages are supported?
We currently support English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Telugu (తెలుగు). You can change your language preference from the App Settings or Profile page.

---

## 👨‍🌾 For Farmers

### 4. What should I do if a worker does not show up?
If an accepted worker does not show up, you can cancel the booking in the app and select "Worker did not show up." This will reset the slot so you can find other workers.

### 5. How do I verify that the worker has completed the hours?
The check-in and check-out timestamps are recorded via QR scanning, showing the precise working duration. You can check these records under the active job details screen.

### 6. Can I hire a group of workers together?
Yes, you can hire a Group Leader who brings their entire pre-registered group. This simplifies communication, attendance, and payments.

---

## 👷 For Workers & Group Leaders

### 7. What happens if the Farmer refuses to pay me?
Dinasari takes wage withholding very seriously. If a farmer refuses to pay the agreed rate, immediately raise a dispute in the app by going to the job history and clicking **Dispute**. Our support team will investigate and restrict the farmer's account if they are found in violation.

### 8. My camera is not working to scan the QR code. What should I do?
Make sure you have granted the Dinasari app permission to access your phone's camera in your device settings. If it still fails, the Farmer can manually check you in using your unique registration ID.

### 9. As a Group Leader, how do I pay my group members?
When you receive the lump-sum payment from the Farmer, it is your responsibility to distribute it to the group members who completed the work. The app displays the payment breakdown per worker to assist you.
