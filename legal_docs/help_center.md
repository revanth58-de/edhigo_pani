# Help Center

Welcome to the **Dinasari Help Center**. We are here to support you at every stage of your farming and agricultural journey. Find step-by-step guides for all roles below.

---

## 👨‍🌾 For Farmers
### 1. How to post a job?
* Open the Dinasari app and select **Farmer Mode**.
* On the Home Screen, choose the crop and work type (e.g., Harvesting, Sowing, Weeding).
* Set the number of workers needed, the daily wage rate (₹), and the job date.
* Tap **Find Workers** to match with individual workers or worker groups nearby.

### 2. How to take attendance?
* When the workers arrive at your farm, open your active job in the app.
* Tap **Check In / Check Out** to display the QR Code.
* Have the worker scan your QR Code with their phone, or scan the leader's group QR code to mark attendance for the team.

### 3. How to complete a job and pay?
* Once the work is done, review the attendance log in the app.
* Tap **Finish Work** to calculate the total amount due.
* Settle the payment directly with the workers or Group Leader via cash or UPI.
* Rate the workers to complete the transaction.

---

## 👷 For Workers
### 1. How to find and accept jobs?
* Switch your profile to **Worker Mode** and set your status to **Online**.
* You will receive notifications for nearby jobs matching your skills.
* Review the work details, location, and daily rate. Tap **Accept** to confirm.

### 2. How do I check in and check out?
* Travel to the farm location shown on the map in the app.
* Upon arrival, ask the Farmer to show their Check-In QR Code.
* Tap **Scan QR** in your app and scan the code. Repeat this process at check-out when the workday ends.

### 3. How do I track my earnings?
* Go to the **Earnings** tab from the bottom navigation or profile page.
* You can view daily, weekly, and monthly earnings summaries and check the status of completed work.

---

## 👑 For Group Leaders
### 1. How do I manage a worker group?
* Open the **Group** tab.
* Tap **Create Group** and add workers by entering their mobile numbers.
* You can manage assignments and accept group job offers on behalf of your entire team.

### 2. How does group attendance work?
* When your group arrives at the farm, show your Group QR code to the Farmer.
* The Farmer will scan it, which checks in all assigned members simultaneously, saving time.
