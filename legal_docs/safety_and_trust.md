# Safety & Trust Guidelines

At **Dinasari**, safety is our top priority. We strive to provide a secure and reliable experience for all members of our agricultural community.

---

## 1. Safety Protocols for Farm Work
Farming can involve hazardous tasks. We advise all users to adhere to these basic safety measures:
* **Heavy Machinery:** Only trained individuals should operate machinery such as tractors, tillers, or harvesters. Ensure safety guards are in place.
* **Chemical Safety:** Wear appropriate protective gear (gloves, masks, long sleeves) when handling or spraying fertilizers and pesticides. Wash hands thoroughly afterwards.
* **Weather Awareness:** Stay hydrated, particularly during hot summer months. Take regular short breaks in the shade to prevent heat stroke.
* **First Aid:** Farmers are encouraged to keep a basic first aid kit (containing bandages, antiseptic lotion, and pain relievers) accessible on the farm.

---

## 2. Platform Trust & Verification
To ensure a secure environment:
* **Profile Verification:** We use mobile OTP authentication to verify that every user is linked to an active mobile number.
* **Review System:** Both farmers and workers rate each other after every job. Honest ratings help keep the community accountable.
* **Zero Underage Labor:** Dinasari strictly prohibits child labor. All users must be at least 18 years of age.

---

## 3. Dealing with Harassment and Abuse
We have a zero-tolerance policy for abuse, threats, or harassment of any kind.
* **Dispute Redressal:** If you face any issues (verbal harassment, unsafe working environments, or wage withholding), use the **Dispute** button on the job page to raise a ticket.
* **Reporting:** You can report abusive users through their profiles or by contacting support at support@dinasari.in.
* **Emergency Helpline:** In case of emergency or immediate physical safety concerns, dial the national emergency number **112** immediately.
