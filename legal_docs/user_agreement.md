# User Agreement and Code of Conduct

**Last Updated:** July 2026

This User Agreement and Code of Conduct establishes the rules, obligations, and codes of conduct governing all users of the **Dinasari** platform. By accessing or using the platform, you agree to comply with this Agreement.

---

## 1. Core Principles
Dinasari is built on mutual respect, honesty, and safety. All users agree to:
* **Zero Discrimination:** Treat all members of the farming community with dignity. There is zero tolerance for discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, age, language, region, or physical ability.
* **No Child Labor:** In compliance with the **Child and Adolescent Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, 1986**, no child under the age of 14 may be hired, registered, or allowed to perform work. Group Leaders must verify that all members of their groups are at least 18 years old.
* **Safe Work Environment:** Ensure farms are safe places to work, with access to clean drinking water, adequate break times, and basic first aid.

---

## 2. Farmer Obligations
Farmers agree to the following conditions:
* **Honest Postings:** Post accurate job details including the location of the farm, specific agricultural work type, timings, and daily payment rates.
* **Fair Payments:** Pay workers and group leaders the full agreed-upon amount promptly upon check-out and work completion. Underpaying or refusing to pay is a breach of this contract and will lead to account termination.
* **Workplace Safety:** Provide safe working conditions, particularly when workers are operating heavy machinery, spraying pesticides, or working in extreme heat.

---

## 3. Worker Obligations
Workers agree to the following conditions:
* **Reliability:** Show up on time for accepted jobs. If you cannot attend due to an emergency, notify the Farmer or Group Leader immediately.
* **QR Attendance:** Perform QR check-in when arriving at the farm and check-out when leaving. This verified record is used for payment tracking.
* **Quality of Work:** Perform your agricultural duties in a diligent and professional manner.

---

## 4. Group Leader Obligations
Group Leaders are subject to additional responsibilities:
* **Accurate Representation:** Register only genuine workers in your group. Ensure all group members have consented to join your group.
* **Attendance Management:** Scan the Group QR attendance correctly for all members who actually worked. Do not check-in members who are absent.
* **Fair Distribution of Earnings:** Distribute the payments received from the Farmer fairly to all group members who participated, based on your group's internal agreements.

---

## 5. Violations and Account Suspension
We reserve the right to suspend or permanently block accounts of users who violate this agreement. Examples of violations include:
* Creating fake jobs or fake worker profiles.
* Chronic cancellations of accepted jobs.
* Verbal or physical abuse, harassment, or threats.
* Withholding wages or failing to pay for completed work.
* Encouraging or facilitating child labor.
