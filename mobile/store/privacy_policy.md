## 1. Introduction

Dinasari ("we", "our", "us") is a platform connecting agricultural workers with farmers in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Dinasari mobile application ("App").

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Phone number**: Used for OTP-based authentication. We do not store full SMS content.
- **Profile information**: Name, role (farmer/worker/leader), work type preferences.
- **Work history**: Job records, attendance, payment history within the platform.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Location data**: Collected when you use the app to match workers with nearby farmers or provide navigation. Location is only active when you explicitly use location features.
- **Device information**: Device type, operating system version, app version — used for crash diagnostics.
- **Crash and error reports**: Anonymized crash logs sent to Sentry (sentry.io) to improve app stability. Phone numbers and OTPs are never included in crash reports.

### 2.3 Camera Access
- The camera is used **only** to scan QR codes for attendance tracking. We do not record video or take photos.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Authentication**: To verify your identity via OTP.
- **Core functionality**: To match workers with farmers, track attendance, process payments.
- **Navigation**: To provide directions from worker to farm location.
- **Push notifications**: To alert you of job offers, arrival confirmations, payment updates.
- **Crash monitoring**: To detect and fix bugs, improving app stability.
- **Analytics**: Aggregated, anonymized data to understand app performance.

---

## 4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only with:
- **Service providers** (Sentry for crash reporting, Google for Maps) - bound by their own privacy policies.
- **Other users on the platform** - your name and role are visible to the party you are matched with (farmer or worker) to facilitate the work transaction.
- **Legal authorities** - if required by law.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- Your data is stored on servers located in India.
- Authentication tokens are stored securely using Android's Keystore-backed secure storage (expo-secure-store).
- We use HTTPS for all communications between the app and our servers.

---

## 6. Location Data

- Location is accessed **only while you are using the app** (foreground only).
- We do not share your precise GPS coordinates with third parties beyond Google Maps for route rendering.
- You can revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

---

## 7. Retention

- Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
- You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at privacy@dinasari.co.in.
- Anonymized crash/analytics data may be retained for up to 2 years.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Dinasari is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

---

## 9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Request correction or deletion of your data.
- Withdraw consent for location access or notifications at any time via device settings.

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@dinasari.co.in
